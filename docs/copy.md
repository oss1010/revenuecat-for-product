## 4. Change it without a release
Headline: Your paywall shouldn't wait for a release.
Body: Build paywalls in a visual editor, start from a template, or
describe one to the AI Editor and refine the draft. Add the SDK once,
then change paywalls and the plans you offer from the dashboard. One
release, then none.
Proof points:
- Visual editor and pre-built templates
- AI Editor for first drafts, from a prompt or a screenshot
- Publish without an app update

## 6. Fit the offer to every user
Headline: Different users. Different offers.
Body: Target paywalls by country, platform, app version or your own
attributes. Build web funnels that route visitors by their answers,
take payment on the web, and give them a link that unlocks their
subscription in the app.
Flow labels (flow tagged Illustrative): Ad click > Web funnel:
"What's your goal?" > Checkout: Apple Pay or Google Pay, where
supported > Redemption Link > App opened, subscription active
Tag on the funnel step: Localized to the visitor's country
