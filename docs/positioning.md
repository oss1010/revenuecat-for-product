# Positioning: RevenueCat for Product Managers

Status: DRAFT v0.1. Not locked. Under agent review.

## The one idea
Own your monetization decisions. Make them yourself, and make the
right ones.

## Positioning statement
For product managers who own subscription revenue, RevenueCat is the
monetization platform that lets you change paywalls, packaging and
targeting yourself, with no app release and no engineering sprint, and
when you test, forecasts which variant will earn more over 12 months
rather than which converted today. Unlike tools you layer on top or
migrate to, it's the platform more than 146,000 apps already run their
subscription revenue on, so the paywall, the experiment and the revenue
number come from the same data.

## Pillar 1: Control (make the decision yourself)
- Change paywalls, packaging and targeting with no app release and no
  engineering sprint
- Speed is the consequence: no queue, no dependency on another team
- Across every surface: in-app and web
- Proof: Paywalls, Targeting, Web Billing, Funnels

## Pillar 2: Impact (make the right decision)
- Judge changes on forecast 12-month revenue, not short-term conversion
- RevenueCat surfaces the likely winner; you ship it in one action
- Proof: Experiments with predicted 12-month LTV winners, one-action
  winner rollout, paywall performance charts, LTV prediction

## The shift we're naming
- Before: pick the paywall that converted, wait for a release to
  change it
- Now: see the likely 12-month value of each variant, ship the winner
  in one action, no release

## Reason to believe
- Scale: 146K+ apps supported, $17B+ revenue processed
  (revenuecat.com, September 2026)
- One system: billing, experiments and analytics on the same data
- For existing customers: the growth tools run on the platform
  engineering already integrated. No new vendor, no migration

## Category
Monetization platform.

## Deliberate choices
- Human decides, machine recommends. We do not claim autopilot: it is
  an Adapty product name, and RevenueCat's rollout appears to be
  human-triggered (claim-auditor to verify).
- Completeness is proof, not the pitch. Adapty, Apphud and Qonversion
  are also full-stack. The defensible difference is being the platform
  most of the market already runs on.
- "Already installed" is a reason to believe, not the lead. Leading
  with it would position a category leader like a challenger.

## Open risks
- "Control" is the message Superwall owns on its PM page. Our version
  must be demonstrably broader: every surface, judged on 12-month
  revenue.
- Superwall attacks RevenueCat's pricing model. The page cannot answer
  this.
- Unknown share of /for-product visitors are existing customers.

## Objections to prepare for
- "Our experimentation stack already does this." It doesn't see
  store-level revenue.
- "Engineering can build it." Maintenance tax and yearly platform
  changes.
- "Superwall is built for PMs." To test.
